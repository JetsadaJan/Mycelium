## Why You Should Contact Us Before Commercial Use

To prevent unauthorized commercial exploitation while keeping the code open for individual learners and hackers, [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) includes a **Self-Executing Commercial Royalty Fallback (Part II)**:

- **Unnegotiated Commercial Rate**: **25% of Gross Revenue** or **$2,500 USD per month** (whichever is greater), plus quarterly financial audit rights and a 20% non-reporting surcharge.
- **Pre-Negotiated Custom License (Recommended)**: When you contact the copyright holder **before** commercial deployment, we offer reasonable startup/enterprise seat pricing or low single-digit custom royalty agreements tailored to your business model.

Before deploying Mycelium in a commercial team, embedding it into a commercial product, or offering it as a hosted service, open a Commercial Inquiry or contact the copyright holder directly:

- **Licensor / Copyright Holder**: **Jetsada Janpaijet**
- **Repository Inquiry**: Open an issue titled `[Commercial License Inquiry] <Company Name>` or reach out via the contact details on the author's GitHub profile.

Include:
1. Organization name and jurisdiction
2. Intended use case (Internal Team Wiki vs. Embedded Product vs. Hosted SaaS)
3. Estimated number of seats or projected annual revenue tier
