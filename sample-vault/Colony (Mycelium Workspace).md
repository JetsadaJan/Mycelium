# Colony (Mycelium Workspace)

Welcome to **Mycelium**—a personal knowledge studio inspired by the biological [[Mycelium Network]] of [[Fungi]] and the associative structure of a [[Biological Neural Network]]. Every folder of Markdown (`.md`) files you open is called a **Colony** and remains 100% **Obsidian Compat** and Git-ready.

## The Four Core Concepts of Mycelium

1. **Colony (Workspace Folder)**: Your local `.md` directory. Mycelium remembers all Colonies you have opened so you can switch between research bases in one click.
2. **[[Node (Mycelium Document)|Node]] (Knowledge Page)**: Each individual `.md` article in the Colony. You can read Nodes in a clean encyclopedia layout or switch to **Write Node** to edit directly in place.
3. **[[Hypha (Mycelium Engine)|Hypha]] (Directional Connection)**: Every `[[Link]]` between Nodes. The right-hand inspector tracks **Incoming Hyphae** (what points here), **Outgoing Hyphae** (what this points to), and **Latent Hyphae** (unlinked words that match existing Nodes).
4. **Cluster (`#tag` Subgroup)**: Topical `#tags` (such as `#biology`, `#neuroscience`, `#ai`, or `#mycelium-system`) that group related Nodes and filter both the sidebar and the interactive Colony Map.

## Epistemic AI Coloring

Whenever an [[Artificial Neural Network]] or coding agent adds information to a Node, it is marked in **Warm Amber** (`> [!ai]` or `==[AI] ...==`). Once you verify the facts, click **Verify** to convert it into standard verified text.
