# Fungi

**Fungi** form a distinct eukaryotic [[kingdom]] encompassing mushrooms, molds, yeasts, and mycorrhizal symbionts. Closer genetically to animals than to plants, fungi construct rigid cell walls out of **chitin** and digest complex organic matter externally before absorbing nutrients through their [[Mycelium Network]].

## Core Life Cycle & Ecological Functions

1. **Spore Dispersal**: Fruiting bodies (mushrooms) release millions of airborne [[Spore|Spores]] capable of surviving extreme environments.
2. **Hyphal Germination**: Each germinating spore extends a primary [[Hypha (Biology)|Hypha]], branching outward to form a new colony.
3. **Forest Symbiosis**: Over 90% of vascular land plants depend on [[Mycorrhiza]] partnerships with fungi to absorb phosphorus, nitrogen, and water.
4. **Adaptive Intelligence**: Without a central brain, fungal colonies solve spatial routing puzzles in ways that parallel a [[Biological Neural Network]].
