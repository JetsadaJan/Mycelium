# Hypha (Biology)

**Hyphae** (singular: **hypha**) are the microscopic, branching cylindrical filaments that collectively make up the [[Mycelium Network]] of [[Fungi]]. Structurally and functionally, biological hyphae behave like the axons and dendrites of a [[Biological Neural Network]], transporting both biochemical cargo and electrical pulses across long distances.

## Cellular Mechanics & Tip Growth

- **Apical Extension**: Growth occurs strictly at the tip, guided by a vesicle-organizing center called the *Spitzenkörper*.
- **Anastomosis (Self-Fusion)**: When two compatible hyphae meet, they fuse walls to create interconnected loops, transforming a simple tree into a resilient graph of [[Node (Graph & Neural)|Nodes]] and edges.
- **Spore Origin**: Every hyphal network begins when a single [[Spore]] hydrates and extends its first germ tube.
