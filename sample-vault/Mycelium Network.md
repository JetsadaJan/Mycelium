# Mycelium Network

**Mycelium** is the vegetative structure of [[Fungi]], consisting of a branching, thread-like mass of [[Hypha (Biology)|Hyphae]] that spreads through soil and organic substrates. Beyond nutrient absorption, mycelial networks act as biological communication conduits connecting forest ecosystems via [[Mycorrhiza]].
