# Mycorrhiza

**Mycorrhiza** is a mutualistic symbiotic association between [[Fungi]] and plant root systems. Host plants supply photosynthetically derived carbohydrates to the fungus, while the [[Mycelium Network]] vastly expands root surface area for water, nitrogen, and phosphate uptake.

## Major Structural Classes

- **Ectomycorrhiza**: [[Hypha (Biology)|Hyphae]] form an external sheath (Hartig net) surrounding root epidermal cells without penetrating interior cell walls.
- **Arbuscular Mycorrhiza (Endomycorrhiza)**: Hyphal tips invaginate root cortical cells to form finely branched arbuscules for nutrient exchange.
- **Signal & Carbon Routing**: Through shared mycorrhizal junctions, trees form an underground graph of [[Node (Graph & Neural)|Nodes]] analogous to a [[Biological Neural Network]].
