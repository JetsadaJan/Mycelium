# Spore

A **Spore** is a microscopic reproductive cell produced by [[Fungi]] that functions as both a dispersal vehicle and a survival capsule. Unlike plant seeds, fungal spores contain no pre-formed embryo; instead, each spore carries the genetic blueprint to nucleate an entirely new [[Mycelium Network]].

## From Single Spore to Interconnected Colony

1. **Airborne & Soil Dispersal**: A single mushroom cap can release billions of spores carried by wind currents or forest insects.
2. **Germination**: Upon landing on moist substrate, the spore swells and extends a nascent [[Hypha (Biology)|Hypha]].
3. **Colony Formation**: As multiple hyphae branch and fuse (anastomosis), they establish a distributed fungal colony capable of forming [[Mycorrhiza]] with nearby plant roots.

In the [[Colony (Mycelium Workspace)|Mycelium software]], creating a new `.md` [[Node (Mycelium Document)|Node]] acts like planting a spore: once connected via [[Hypha (Mycelium Engine)|Hyphae]], it grows into part of the wider knowledge cluster.
